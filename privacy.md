# PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Podania" Discord bot ("Bot"), managed by kafixonek ("Bot Owner"), collects, uses, and protects user data.

1. Data Collection: The Bot may process data provided by Discord (such as User IDs, usernames, and server IDs) solely for the purpose of processing recruitment forms and applications ("podania") on your server.
2. Data Storage: No personal data or message content is sold or shared with third parties. Data is stored securely and only as long as necessary for the Bot to function.
3. User Rights: Users can request the removal of their data processed by the Bot at any time by contacting the Bot Owner or removing the Bot from the server.
4. Contact: For any privacy concerns, contact Krzysztof Buczyński through Discord.
